Meet Ary

Give your curiosity a voice
and explore.

Ary la Cotorra is a robotic cultural and tourism assistant. Her purpose: to help visitors discover the Dominican Republic through their own interests.

A personality that invites conversation.
A friendly, curious parrot with a Dominican identity. An inviting place to ask questions, even when you do not know exactly what to look for.
A recommendation you can picture.
The proposed experience combines a conversational answer with images of the place, food or story the visitor is discovering.
A purpose beyond giving directions.
Bringing Dominican culture closer to travellers and sparking interest in possibilities they have yet to discover.

01 / The experience

“I ended up knowing the hotel buffet better than my own country”.

Ten days on holiday in the Dominican Republic. Eager to explore, but the same question every morning: “what shall we do today?”. If it can happen to someone from here, it can also happen to a first-time visitor.

That is how the idea of Ary began: giving visitors someone to ask and a new reason to go out and discover.

02 / The goal

Give curiosity a place to start.

We do not all look for the same things when we travel. Some want to try a dish, others to learn a town's history or find somewhere to spend the afternoon.

We want visitors to chat with Ary, see what they are talking about and leave with a clearer idea of what they would like to discover. Technology serves that moment: “I want to go there!”.

03 / The opportunity

A welcome that invites exploration.

For an airline such as Arajet, the proposal would be to welcome arriving travellers with an experience connecting the flight to the destination. For a restaurant, to spark interest in the flavours and stories behind the food.

The next step would be to explore a pilot: a venue, relevant content and a way to observe whether the conversation helps visitors discover something new.

Commercial proposal under development. Agreements, results and economic benefits are not presented as confirmed facts.

About us

Technology rooted
in our culture.

We are the team behind Ary: a project bringing together robotics, conversation and Dominican culture. Our story began with Don Rafael, a doll with Arduino and sensors, and continues with a parrot inviting people to discover our culture.

You do not need to know every answer to start exploring. Sometimes you only need to know whom to ask.

Our motivation is to make sure the desire to discover the Dominican Republic goes beyond “what shall we do now?”.

The WRO presentation focuses on a familiar problem, an experience that is easy to understand and a proposal for use in real settings.
